Listening pill: five redesigns

The pill is the small bar at the top of the screen while you dictate. Each design below is animated with a made-up sound wave and text, so you can see how it feels.

All five can be built with the app's current drawing code. Real blur behind the window isn't possible, so "glass" looks become a dark, slightly see-through background.

1 · Capsule closest to now

A fully rounded capsule. On the left, a red mic button with a soft pulse ring. In the middle, the newest words. On the right, a timer. The sound wave sits inside the mic button's pulse instead of taking up space.

3 · Island

A black pill, inspired by the iPhone's "Dynamic Island". It starts small (dot and timer) and smoothly stretches open as words arrive, then shrinks back when done. It feels like part of the system.
