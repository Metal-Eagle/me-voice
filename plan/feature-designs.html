2Silence trimming & auto-stop

New rows on Behaviour. Silence is cut before transcription (faster, and no made-up "Thank you." on quiet audio). Auto-stop is optional, for when you tap to start and forget to tap again.

The countdown ring appears only in the last 2 seconds before auto-stop.

  • Start with an energy gate in audio.rs (the level meter already exists); Silero VAD as a later swap if needed.
  • Trimming also shortens what the live preview has to re-decode, so the preview keeps up on long dictations.
